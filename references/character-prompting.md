# Character Bible Construction Formula

To prevent face-drift across multi-scene generations:
`[Anchor Subject], [Age/Ethnicity], [Facial Features], [Hair/Grooming], [Exact Clothing], [Lighting/Environment], [Camera Focal Length]`

Example:
"32-year-old Rwandan executive, sharp jawline, short fade hairstyle, wearing a charcoal tailored suit with metallic gold lapel pin, soft rim lighting, shallow depth of field, 85mm portrait lens, photorealistic 8k, consistent facial geometry."
